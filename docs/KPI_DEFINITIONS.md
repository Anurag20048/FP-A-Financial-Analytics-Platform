# FP&A KPI Definitions

| KPI | Definition | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Actual Revenue | Sum of actual revenue | Revenue achieved in the reporting dataset |
| Budgeted Revenue | Sum of budgeted revenue | Planned revenue |
| Revenue Variance | Actual Revenue - Budgeted Revenue | Positive = above revenue budget |
| Revenue Variance % | Revenue Variance / Budgeted Revenue | Relative revenue performance |
| Actual Cost | Sum of actual cost | Actual incurred cost in the dataset |
| Cost Variance | Actual Cost - Budgeted Cost | Positive = cost overrun |
| Gross Profit | Actual Revenue - Actual Cost | Revenue remaining after modeled cost |
| Gross Margin % | Gross Profit / Actual Revenue | Gross profit as a percentage of revenue |
| Budget Attainment % | Actual Revenue / Budgeted Revenue | Percentage of revenue budget achieved |
| Gross Profit Variance | Actual Gross Profit - Budgeted Gross Profit | Profitability performance vs plan |

## Variance convention

Revenue variance is treated as favorable when actual revenue is at or above budget.
Cost variance is treated as favorable when actual cost is at or below budget.

Always validate the source definitions with the business before using these measures in a production finance process.
